How to play

Move every card to the four foundations at the top right, building each suit from Ace up to King.

On the table, stack cards downward in alternating colors. The four free cells at top left each hold one card.

Moves make a soft pop. You can record your own sound in the Menu instead; it stays on this device.

Today’s deal is the same for everyone on the same date, so you can compare scores with friends. Solve it on consecutive days to build a daily streak.

Your score starts at 10,000 and drops 40 points per move and 2 points per second, so one move costs the same as 20 seconds. Fewer moves matter most.

Review replays your game move by move and rates each move, like a chess review, with a better option where there was one. Hints always follow a line that leads to a win. Your moves stay on this device and are forgotten when you start a new deal.

The Scene button randomizes the animated background: new colors and new shapes every time.

Tap a card once and it moves to the best spot: a foundation first, then a matching column, then a free cell. To choose the spot yourself, drag the card or run of cards. Moving a run needs enough free cells and empty columns.
